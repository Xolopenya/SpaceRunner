## Limits and failure interpretation

A certificate proves one modeled route from a snapshot through the complete supplied static field and terminal reservation. It does not prove arbitrary-input safety, search completeness, discoverability, human readability, infinite survival or universal absence of bypasses. A search/publication rejection is not proof that no physical route exists.

The pressure policy can produce more vetoes when a historical control proposal no longer clears an encounter at the actual publication root. Increasing proposal counts cannot compensate for those omitted spans. See AB_COMPARISON for measured acceptance and corner probes. No additional retry/search, forced player movement, future-input oracle or relaxed gate is used to rescue B.

Threat counts use a labeled proxy:distinct published hazards contacted on a1.5s zero-steering continuation, checked every0.1s using residual velocity and actual calibrated CCD. This indicates that braking along that continuation would meet a hazard; it is not a measured human decision or proof of a uniquely required maneuver. Raw prescribed collisions are distinguished from real three-hit death and post-death research continuation.

Proximity/threat/unique-contact research queries use the complete known logical field, including conservatively retained sources after visual disappearance on damage. Actual hull events additionally require an active render/collision slot and the unchanged immunity clock. These logical metrics therefore must not be relabeled as additional real damage or visible-hazard events; `hitEvents`/`death` provide that separate evidence.
