# Spawn and pacing

The DEV overlay reuses the mixed-flow scheduler/cursor and original ordinary/wreck builders. After two ordinary decisions, seeded mine selection has50% probability, with a bounded fourth-decision maximum when the selected section is eligible. A station-wreck decision is retained rather than replaced. A mine owns six24-unit cells and replaces only its whole unpublished encounter. Following decisions retain existing director pacing/family history and aperture bag; there is no global seconds timer.

Frequency constants live in `experiments/moving-mine/settings.ts`. Fixed overrides select the same behavior's speed/direction. Default seed42 first proposes/publishes a slow right-to-left mine in section starting at384m; pass distance468m. Precommit witness-followed actual publication is265.7426747685m, around12.8s; motion starts tick2159 (17.9917s) and the ship-plane crossing occurs around21.99s. Publication is input-dependent and can be vetoed; seed alone reproduces proposals rather than every accepted world.

For immediate repeatable testing use `?debug=field&experiment=moving-mine&seed=42&firstMine=1&mine=slow-ltr`. The first transaction spans96–240m and the mine crosses the ship plane at180m, approximately8.78s. Ordinary original asteroids precede it in the same transaction. Its source remains certified, so deliberately bad inputs can still veto it.

The benchmark counts selected mine proposals, actual mine publications, explicit omissions/fallbacks and all attempt reasons. It records first publication/pass distances and intervals between published mines. Across15 actual moving-model runs:48 proposals,43 mine publications,5 omissions. Seed42 witness flight publishes8/8 mine proposals through3000m, with10.05–19.10 simulation seconds between publications. In actual blind seed42 tapes: horizontal2/2, boundary3/3, touch2/3 and neutral1/3 before their recorded run ends. These are different accepted fields and finite runs, not a universal frequency guarantee. The on-screen recent counters cover the bounded64-event ring; benchmark totals retain the complete finite run.

There is one active/known mine maximum. Source spacing is conservative and structural admission rejects a second overlapping mine even if later tuning violates this assumption. No swarm, mine/bulkhead compound or mine/narrow-gate transaction exists. Rejection uses the existing whole certified ambient fallback, retaining all original committed geometry. Fallback/uncertified omissions are separately visible in raw outcomes.

The available decision is choosing XY position at the mine's future crossing: stay on the side it will leave, cross its swept band earlier/later by steering laterally, or avoid the band vertically. The player cannot stop forward travel; 'waiting' means retaining a safe lateral position before changing sides. Before/after options depend on current speed/position and are not guaranteed in every configuration. A steady red marker and visible constant motion provide the only telegraph; no HUD alarm or audio system is added.
