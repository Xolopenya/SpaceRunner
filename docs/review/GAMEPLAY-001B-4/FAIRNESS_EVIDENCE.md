## What is certified

A committed outcome contains a complete candidate plus a replayed witness from the matched actual publication root through the supplied complete known field into its new empty tail. It certifies those inputs under the implemented model. It does not certify arbitrary human inputs, complete continuous-state reachability, visual discoverability or endless survival.

The session's primary normal validation already independently replays its base and union. A separate fresh replay checks the accepted full witness/hash. Publication replays from a fixed-tick forecast; actual root and known field must match at that tick. Completing an incremental replay early never authorizes stale publication later. Director-v1 now explicitly normalizes progression, probability and intensity to12 decimal places. Initial raw differences and failed log are retained, and the exact Node/Chrome assertions remain enabled.
