## Limits

The replay is conditional on a static in-process owned field; there is no network authority or adversarial mutation guarantee. Combined keyboard+touch can produce raw components outside the approved validator domain (absolute component greater than1). Original steering still receives that input; planner admission fails closed. This requires an explicit future input-contract decision rather than silently changing physics.

Actors in the longer feasibility corpus continue after contacts to study publication vetoes. Contact substeps/unique hazards are not real hull events or survival after death. Real hull lifecycle is tested separately. Sampled routes and zero failed audits do not prove human readability, no permanent bypass, infinite safety, or completeness of bounded search.
