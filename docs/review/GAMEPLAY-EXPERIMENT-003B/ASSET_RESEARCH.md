# Asset research — GAMEPLAY-EXPERIMENT-003B

Research date: 2026-10-25. Only original author/license pages were used; no mirror download or third-party script was executed.

| Candidate | Author / original source | Verified license / format | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Space Kit | [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit) | Author page: CC0, 150 assets. The existing project subset is original GLB. | Existing catalog has two round meteors, half meteor, barrel, generator and ship. None is an aperture structure. Scaling these meshes would preserve the wrong collision topology. |
| Space Station Kit | [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-station-kit) | Author page: CC0, 90 assets, released2024. Individual source files/formats were not downloaded or measured. | Modular interior kit is a plausible future art source; no single correctly proportioned bulkhead mesh was established in this investigation. No suitability/geometry claim is made from the license alone. |
| Ultimate Modular Sci-Fi Pack | [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularscifi.html) | Author page: CC0; 46 models, FBX/OBJ/Blend. | Modular interiors/props require selection, conversion and geometry calibration. No verified single target mesh was established; no download added. |

[Kenney support](https://kenney.nl/support) confirms commercial use and optional attribution for these assets. [Creative Commons CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits copying, modification and distribution, including commercial use. These checks establish the stated pack licenses, not that every pack contains a certified flight aperture. No claim that the entire internet lacks a suitable model is made.

The task's permitted custom-geometry fallback was selected to keep this experiment to one controllable profile. **Abandoned Docking Bulkhead v1** is original project geometry authored by Codex for Space Runner, not a modified Kenney mesh. Original format: TypeScript / Three.js BoxGeometry and flush PlaneGeometry markings. Four connected solid members and orange aperture lining are authored directly from the collision contract; there are no invisible filler boxes, downloaded textures, library installation or GLB conversion. Original model source, source URL and CC0 dedication are in `assets/experimental/bulkhead/PROVENANCE.md` and `LICENSE.txt`. Model attribution is optional; existing Three.js MIT and Kenney notices remain intact. New GLB bytes/network requests: **0**.

This choice does not present primitive geometry as replacement art for the existing ship or asteroid field. It is the single explicitly requested structural prototype, isolated from production.
