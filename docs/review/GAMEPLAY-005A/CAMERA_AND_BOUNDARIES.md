# Selected camera and arena presentation

Ordinary Play selects the approved CAMERA-001 Hybrid Chase:80% ship X/Y translation applied coherently to both camera and target. FOV58°, look depth−28, near/far planes and aspect-driven rear distance remain unchanged. Reset retains Hybrid. There is no public A/B/C control. Historical `debug=camera` still offers the comparison; historical collectible diagnostics keep their original camera for reproducibility. This selection applies to this integration branch, pending owner approval for any future main change.

`ArenaBoundaries` replaces the old nine very faint corner frames with four sparse world-space edge streams. Each edge has sixteen short segments distributed across128m depth. Four geometries/materials are allocated once. Ordinary depth testing remains active; the cue cannot draw through hazards, and it writes no depth. There are no opaque surfaces, extra lights, postprocessing or particles to collide with.

Edge opacity is.035 in the center and rises quadratically to.24 over the nearest1.8 movement units. Left/right and upper/lower edges respond independently; the opposite edge stays faint. Lines sit1.3 units outside the center limits as a visual envelope, not a collision tolerance measurement. The stream moves only with run distance and therefore freezes during pause. Hybrid leaves it world anchored, helping distinguish ship motion from the arena perimeter.

The renderer receives X/Y/distance scalars only. It cannot change Ship, obstacles, fragments, mine state or RNG. Unit coverage verifies central opacity on all four edges and stronger right/lower responses, stationary paused data, reset, disposal and unchanged serialized simulation. Browser coverage uses real keyboard movement to approach the right edge and checks portrait optics. Clean and collider screenshots include the same recorded positions. Exact wing clearance still depends on the existing unbanked sphere profile, while the mesh banks cosmetically; the cue does not remove that approximation.
