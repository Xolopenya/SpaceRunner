# Matched production visual review

Open `visuals.html` in the ZIP. Every pair is labelled001B baseline /001B1 final;
click for the original full-size PNG. The two sets each contain38 frames, six silent
native browser videos and JSON receipts. The same seed42 inputs, original published
hazards, Hybrid camera, viewport, tick and post-contact times are used. Neither side
inserts hazards or changes HP/cooldowns. Prefixes are accelerated and screenshot
states deliberately held; post-contact advancement uses native RAF. This is an
automated playable-session capture, not an owner playtest or listening test.

The shield's dissipation sample is already beyond the1.6s authoritative cooldown:
its absence is expected, not a missing effect. Desktop/portrait camera and ship pose
vary naturally during the same flight; baseline/final counterparts match exactly.

Observed changes from the actual camera: the cap's triangular wireframe is gone;
banked field edges and the initial ripple leave existing damage fissures readable.
The mine's uninterrupted flat circle becomes sparse curved contours at different
depths around a short cyan release. Its footprint remains smaller than the ship's
catastrophic release. Ship plasma emerges above the separating hull, with warm
component flares and fragment-linked electrical jets; later frames lose energy
while recognizable wings/engine/nose continue drifting before results.

Iteration1 was too faint and read as a soft continuous bubble. Reference frames
are retained separately. Edge contrast was increased; wave surfaces were broken
into thin arcs, haze silhouette was suppressed, and primary ship plasma was moved
slightly toward the visible engine bay. The final short warm peak remains bounded
around the ship and reveals the larger pieces throughout breakup.

Limits: these are economical procedural surfaces, not volumetric fluid simulation.
Fine ripples/sparks are small in portrait. Hull partitions still have open edges;
the reverse-face material improves interiors without claiming watertight caps.
Inherited HUD text overlap remains outside this patch. Physical phones, subjective
comfort and the owner's artistic acceptance of001B1 remain unverified. Visual quality
is supported by inspected frames/recordings, not conclusively established by tests.
