# Collectible design

Baseline10cc436 has eight fixed slots, one reward per shard and one bounded
proposal after successful ordinary publications. Its initial threshold is2
ordinary sections, then2–4 (mean3). An occasional usable station aperture can
replace a later ordinary opportunity after at least one ordinary section. Failed
placement retries on later admitted content. There is no separate timer spawner.

The existing placement system is retained: seeded identity hash, admitted hazard
anchors, bounds/lead checks, natural-route exclusion, full body/wing pickup pose,
local approach clearance, active-shard separation and optional station openings.
`naturalRouteOffset=1.55`, approachDepth8m, separation12m, visible shard radius.54m,
pickup radius.32m and boundary inset1.1m remain unchanged. No automatic safe lane.

## Three bounded templates

Each upgrade has exactly three shards22m apart, spanning44m. At20–36.72m/s the
nominal interval is1.10–.60s, longer than the unchanged140ms attraction. They share
the admitted anchor and section; IDs append `/trail/0..2`. Later members may outlive
the anchor hazard, but remain in its already admitted section. Reveal birth still
uses the existing successful-publication receipt. The crystal model/view and
ordinary pickup audio/VFX are unchanged.

- Straight: fixed X/Y, rewarding maintenance of a chosen trajectory.
- Curve: hash-selected horizontal/vertical direction, three samples of a mild
  parabola with2.4m total transverse change. The longest hop is1.8m; at maximum
  forward speed it needs about3.0m/s, below8m/s lateral maximum. The default velocity
  change is comfortably below32m/s² acceleration. A runtime hop-speed guard caps
  optional templates to half the lateral maximum.
- Offset: straight at least2.6m from the publication-time braking/coast endpoint.
  This is a positional challenge rather than a fourth rendering shape. It needs
  intentional movement; it never tracks the ship after admission.

All template points obey the original natural-route/assist exclusion and current
root reachability estimate. The whole short full-profile path, including8m entry/
exit, is swept against known geometry with.28m clearance. Ordinary rotating objects
use their enclosing sphere; station checks use every actual solid. The path remains
inside the admitted section with10m endpoint margins. A known moving hazard at
this depth suppresses the upgrade rather than forecasting its pursuit. A failed
template or insufficient free slots leaves the original valid single shard.

This is a conservative local route check and lead estimate, not a certified global
solution from every future player state. New players can still choose a risky
entry, steer imperfectly or collect only part of a sequence. The actual pickup
continues to sweep the three spheres and reject obstruction through solids.

## Cadence and economy

Ordinary thresholds are now `[2,3,3,3]`, mean2.75, nominally9.1% more frequent than
mean3. The suggested15–20% increase was treated as a starting point; a restrained
cadence was chosen because some opportunities now contain three rewards. Actual
successful groups and individual shards are measured separately, including failed
candidate retries and aperture replacement.

Each block of12 successful groups has one hash-selected eligible upgrade slot.
There is at most one triple per block, at most14 shards per12 groups. Rejected
upgrades do not accumulate or catch up later. There is no bonus credit, bootstrap
stock or reward multiplier. Ignoring aperture/rejection effects, the combined
nominal long-run upper mean is about27.3% above the old isolated schedule; the
fixed-input measured sample is+12% individual density/+4% groups. Safety/pool caps
can reduce it. Short prefixes can contain a visible three-shard burst; they are
not a uniform density guarantee. The fixed pool remains8, preserving quiet gaps.
