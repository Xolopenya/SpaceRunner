## Observed presentation

Desktop close straight shows ordered cyan shards ahead amid ordinary rocks, with
the same small halo/model. Curve shows three offset points expanding naturally
through perspective as the ship banks to approach. The offset example starts away
from the initial flight line. No forward wing beam or pickup contour remains;
the familiar engine exhaust and warp field are unchanged. Ordinary hazards retain
their silhouettes. Partial/complete clips demonstrate separate member collection.

Portrait curve retains three cyan points and the original compact HUD, though far
shards are small and perspective compresses spacing. A distant member can be
occluded by ordinary debris; existing visibility/obstruction remains. Screenshots
support inspection of ordering and removal of clutter, not a claim that all players
will recognize curves or improve collection rates. Landscape respects the existing
2:1 drawable limit. Mobile/reduced-motion checks preserve lower-cost quality and
the original animation behavior.

The gallery uses manual controls, held image pairs and baseline/final video pairs
where present. The two sources replay the same controls; initial loading/prefix
segments make media timestamps approximate rather than frame-synchronized. Use
the held states for exact spatial comparison. Native gallery loading, playback and
desktop/portrait overflow checks accompany delivery.

Owner/new-player readability, satisfaction, desired trail rarity and physical phone
visibility still need playtesting. No human results are fabricated and no visual
size, steering or permanent navigation aid is introduced to force success.
