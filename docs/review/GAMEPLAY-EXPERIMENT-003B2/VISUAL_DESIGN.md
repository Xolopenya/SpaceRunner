# Salvaged station frame — visual design

The gray barrier is replaced with an original composed station pressure-frame fragment: four connected steel members with asymmetric top/bottom steps and different depths. A dark port spine carries a seven stencil; pale salvaged machinery plating contrasts with the rust crown and keel. Broad panel seams, uneven repair bands, thin scorch scratches and orange inner faces establish industrial scale without a new texture/font library. The aperture is offset inside this fixed silhouette.

Source geometry: `src/experiments/structure-variants/profile.ts` and `view.ts`. Four exact box solids surround the passage; their depths range from3.6 to5.2 units and include explicit Z offsets. Surface paint is batched into indexed meshes with ordered polygon-offset layers. The first paint pass was too tiled and had overlapping coplanar markings; the final pass uses fewer broad panels and distinct depth-bias layers. Every painted vertex is tested to remain on a real solid. Paint scratches are not physical gaps; no decorative protrusion is omitted from the collider and nothing fills the opening.

Approach B is used: original authored members composed into one wreck, with procedural surface markings. Official author pages were checked again: [Kenney Space Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit) and [Space Station Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-station-kit) state CC0; [Quaternius Ultimate Modular Sci-Fi](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularscifi.html) lists46models, CC0 and FBX/OBJ/Blend. No single ready-to-use wreck mesh with calibrated aperture was established. These are candidate-pack license checks, not claims of inspecting every source mesh. No unverified mirror, download, conversion or new large pack was used.

The original v2 model/markings are authored by Codex for Space Runner and dedicated under CC0-1.0. Full provenance/source URL/notice: `assets/experimental/station-wreck-v2/`. Existing Kenney GLBs, Three.js MIT notice and all production assets remain unchanged. No new GLB or network request is added. The model is a source artifact, not an AI-generated screenshot or a replacement for the original ship.

Eight representation groups share all five prebuilt variant templates. Every committed large slot hides its borrowed small GLB and shows its own matching frame; ordinary mixed hazards keep the standard renderer. Geometry/materials are allocated at DEV setup, reused on restart and disposed by their own ResourceOwner. Collider overlay uses those same four per-variant boxes and the unchanged pink player sphere. No camera, light rig, audio or VFX code changes.

Entry and exit/portrait/landscape images are real rendering. The unchanged forgiving ship sphere can permit wing grazing. Lower exits can briefly obstruct the chase-camera view after ship clearance; owner judgment is still required. This is a stronger visual prototype, not a claim that human visual acceptance is complete.
