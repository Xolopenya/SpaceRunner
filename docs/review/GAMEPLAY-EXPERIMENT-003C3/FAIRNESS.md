# Fairness and publication

**Safety supported; H3 availability remains unaccepted, status Partial.** Every accepted target has a fresh proof tied to the exact current publication root, and an independently replayable witness. The finite constructive search is incomplete; no-witness/exhaustion is not mathematical impossibility.

## Unchanged contract

120 Hz actual Ship dynamics; held 54-tick/0.45 s current-input prefix; 0.15 safety margin; 39 neutral braking ticks; 16 terminal reservation ticks; 1800-tick horizon; 3072 propagations/6144 primitive queries per call; 144 planning-call deadline; maximum 2 searches, 4 replays, 2 attempts. Acquisition waiting does not add an extra work budget. Fallbacks consume the same transaction budget, and their actual-root gate replays the newly prepared native witness. No third constructive search is introduced.

Canonical metadata reconstruction, nested freezing, exact stable-ID/ownership equality, source payload membership, all committed hazards, capacity/separation and safe terminal continuation remain mandatory. A stale root, malformed span, cancelled reservation, unresolved target, expired warning, insufficient budget or independent replay collision cannot publish a target. No later retargeting is allowed.

- 1458 engineered observed-root cases: 3 seeds ×3 speeds ×3 minimum warnings ×9 positions(center/four edges/four corners) ×6 velocities. 1458 accepted and independently replayed; 0 resolution rejection, 0 search rejection, 0 unfinished. This is a bounded supplied corpus, not universal reachability.
- 37 Target publications in 45 matched real three-hull games across all policies; all Target captures exactly match their publication contexts. All accepted fixture, grid, ordinary and runtime witnesses are independently replayed: 2236 total new audit records (including 35 unhinted counterfactual accepts).
- Three deliberately late Target evasion tapes reject on actual mine collision. Focused tests additionally cover stale capture, reaction/warning expiry, zero work budget, invalid ownership span, cancellation/deadline, exact phase collision, pause/restart and full mixed geometry.
- Each Target causal certificate follower reaches 3000 m with hull 3 for seeds 0/11/42. Mine publications 6/7, 7/7, 7/8 respectively. No human safety/comfort verdict is inferred.

## Omissions and H3

Target 37/48 published (77.08%), 11/48 omitted (22.92%); Fast and Diagonal 43/48 (89.58%), 5/48 (10.42%). These are per-policy 15 normal histories, whose later lengths/geometry diverge with actual deaths; all proposals and root traces are retained.

Ten Target primary searches fail on already committed geometry (nine wreck members, one native scattered object), before reaching the mine; one resolver rejects TARGET_TOO_CLOSE_TO_ORIGIN after the player moved during acquisition. Final outcomes: 6 NO_WITNESS_FOUND fallback misses, 4 PLANNING_DEADLINE, 1 STALE_PUBLICATION_STATE fallback replay collision. These are bounded planning failures and are not classified as unavoidable mine attacks. The same-source complete fallback/omission is preferable to publishing without proof.

Proposal hints improve two accepted cases without changing the safety authority, but do not solve these 11 failures. The 22.92% omission rate is materially worse than the matched 10.42% reference. Therefore H3's "without excessive omissions" condition is not accepted and the mode stays isolated. Follow-up should improve actual-root route proposals under the SAME caps, with explicit replay/counterfactual evidence, before regular-flow promotion.

Machine-readable contexts, controls, target metadata, attempts, rejection details, job/work counts, causal poses and independent hashes are retained in `logs/target-lock-witnesses.json` and benchmark/browser raw files in the ZIP.
