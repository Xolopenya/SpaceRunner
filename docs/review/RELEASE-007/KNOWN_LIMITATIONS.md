- Sparse warp cues describe ship-center movement limits,not wing-clearance tolerance. Cosmetic banking remains independent from the calibrated arcade collision spheres. Foreground light can project over a distant panel without physically intersecting it.

- Safety audits follow actual certificates or deliberate test inputs;they do not prove every human steering history is safe or judge enjoyment. Four long seeds and the existing broader regression corpus are finite samples.
- Videos are silent native browser captures. Public audio status/settings are exercised and the existing full audio tests measure real output;no claim of new human listening approval.
- Physical phones,Yandex SDK/moderation,backend/economy/combat and GAMEPLAY-005C are outside this release. No new gameplay feature or tuning is introduced by release preparation.
