# Salvaged station pressure-frame v2

Original Space Runner geometry authored by Codex for GAMEPLAY-EXPERIMENT-003B2, 2026-10-25.
Approach: composed authored industrial members (Option B), with procedural surface paint (Option C).
No third-party model, texture, font or asset library was downloaded.

Source: `src/experiments/structure-variants/view.ts` (`wreckModel`) and `profile.ts` (all solid geometry).
Original/final format: TypeScript / Three.js meshes. No GLB conversion.
Source URL: https://github.com/Xolopenya/SpaceRunner/tree/feature/gameplay-003b2-structure-variants/src/experiments/structure-variants

Four connected members have different depths and an asymmetric stepped outer silhouette. Original panel layouts, repair bands, scorch paint, a geometric seven stencil and orange aperture lining use flush meshes. Every painted vertex stays on a real solid. There are no painted ship-sized holes or non-colliding protrusions. The aperture and silhouette are authored from the exact four collision boxes for each of five variants.
